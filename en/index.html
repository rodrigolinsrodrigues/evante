About us

EVANTE brings together researchers from different fields (Education, Computer Science and Psychology) around one central question: what are the real impacts of educational technologies on learning?

To answer it, we carry out scientific experiments and analyses of educational data, producing evidence that supports the decisions of administrators, teachers and researchers. Our work starts from the principle that the adoption of technology in education should be guided by evidence, not by intuition or market trends.

The laboratory is part of the Federal Rural University of Pernambuco (UFRPE) and is registered in the CNPq Directory of Research Groups, Brazil.

Research lines

01

Line 1: Educational Data Science and Learning Analytics

We investigate methods and techniques for collecting, analyzing and interpreting educational data, in order to understand and improve learning and the environments in which it takes place. We develop software components, such as data visualizations and dashboards, for distance, blended and face-to-face education. This line also covers educational data mining algorithms that identify patterns to help educators make decisions and improve learning outcomes.

02

Line 2: Technology-Mediated Teaching Methods and Techniques

We study how different teaching methods and strategies perform when mediated by technology, including active learning, collaborative learning and remote teaching. The focus is on producing evidence about which approaches work, in which contexts and for which student profiles.

03

Line 3: Development and Evaluation of Educational Technologies

We design, build and evaluate technologies for teaching and learning, from platforms and dashboards to games and programming environments. Evaluation follows experimental protocols that measure the real impact of these tools on learning, not just their usability or adoption.

Services

We bring research results into practice, offering data analysis and educational technology services to institutions, administrators and educators.

Educational data analysis consulting

We help administrators and educators extract evidence from educational data, grounding pedagogical decisions, assessments and policies in evidence-based analysis.

Dashboards integrated into virtual learning environments

As the use of virtual learning environments has grown, the volume of data collected has become harder for educators to analyze. We develop dashboard interfaces integrated into these environments that make the data easier to visualize and understand, supporting more effective pedagogical decisions.

Development and evaluation of educational technologies

We design, build and evaluate technologies for teaching and learning (from platforms and dashboards to games and programming environments) using experimental protocols that measure real impact on learning.

Training and capacity building

Workshops and training in Learning Analytics, educational data mining and the pedagogical use of evidence, preparing teams at educational institutions to make data-driven decisions.
